# Portfolio

This portfolio presents Isaiah's work, career history, and ways to get in touch.

## Language

**IF Monogram**

The original visual mark made from Isaiah Francois's initials for the Startup Intro.

**Portfolio Shell**

The macOS-inspired frame around the portfolio's existing pages, including its menu bar and social
dock.

**Startup Intro**

A brief, skippable opening that uses Isaiah's branding and evokes a Mac starting up. It appears
when a tab first opens directly on the homepage without a section link.
